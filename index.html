Routing

One order, several venues, one settlement.

A single pool gets more expensive the deeper you trade into it. Tessera spreads the order so that no leg pushes its venue's price too far.

  1. 1

    Read every venue

    Pool depth and fee tier are pulled for each venue that lists the pair. Stable-swap pools join only when both tokens are stablecoins.

  2. 2

    Cut the order into 5% slices

    Each slice goes to whichever venue returns the most for it, given what earlier slices already took. Venues that fill up get fewer slices.

  3. 3

    Weigh the gas

    Every extra leg costs gas. A split is kept only when the extra output beats the extra network fee.

  4. 4

    Settle atomically

    All legs execute in one transaction. If the total falls below your minimum received, the whole swap reverts.

Safety

Guardrails on every swap.

Slippage floor

You set a max slippage. The contract checks the total output against minOut and reverts if the market moved further than that.

Deadline

A signed swap that sits in the mempool too long expires. The default is 20 minutes and you can change it in settings.

Impact warnings

Price impact above 3% turns amber, and above 15% the swap button locks. Trading that size needs a smaller order or a deeper market.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Is this swapping real tokens?

No. This site is a working demonstration of the routing engine. Prices drift on a timer, the wallet is a simulated account, and swaps only update the balances shown on this page.

Why split an order at all?

Automated market makers price along a curve. The more you buy from one pool, the worse each extra unit gets. Two half-size trades in two pools usually beat one full-size trade in either.

What is price impact versus slippage?

Price impact is how much your own trade moves the price, and it is known when you get the quote. Slippage is how much the market moves between quoting and settlement. Your max slippage caps the second one.

Where does the 0.05% fee go?

In a live deployment it would go to the protocol treasury. It is already included in the quote you see, so the receive amount is what you would get.
